GECO Leaflet Map

This map demonstrates how a gridded surface can be rendered directly in Leaflet from R.

About the layer

The coloured cells are a georeferenced version of R’s built-in volcano matrix. Each pixel stores one elevation value; neighbouring colours reveal the shape and gradient of the surface.

Explore

  • Pan and zoom across the raster.
  • Toggle the raster in the layer control.
  • Use the legend to interpret cell values.
